199class Restore(ShadowClientSubscriberMixin):

201 

202 Hatch sold two first generation Restores. The later one reports itself as 

203 ``restoreIot`` and is handled by RestoreIot. This one is older and lays its 

204 shadow out differently: what is playing lives under ``content``, the sound

207 ``clock`` instead, so the two cannot share a parser.

211 the device only holds the ids of the alarms it should load.
